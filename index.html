Cheque & Voucher Printer

Paste rows from the QuickBooks report, tick the ones you need, print.

1. Load the report

In QuickBooks export "Transactions by Account" to Excel or CSV, then open the file here (or drop it on this box). Columns read: Type, Date, Num, Name, Memo, Split, Amount.

Or paste copied rows:

2. Choose vouchers

DateVoucherPayeeMemoAccountAmountCheque no

3. Print

Combined vouchers list every selected row as a line and the cheque carries the total. Payee names must match exactly to group, so fix spelling in the table first.

4. Fit it to your bank's cheque (all in mm)

These starting values are a guess. Print one on plain paper, hold it against a real cheque to a light, and adjust. Save the result as a preset per bank.

Click the voucher no. or date on a voucher to edit it.
